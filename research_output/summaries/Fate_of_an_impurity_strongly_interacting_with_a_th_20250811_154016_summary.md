# Summary

- **Study Focus**: Investigated the behavior of mobile impurities in a thermal Bose gas, emphasizing the effects of temperature and impurity-bath interaction strength.
- **Key Findings**:
  - Spectra narrow with increasing temperature for strong impurity-bath interactions.
  - Impurity energy shift is suppressed compared to a quasipure Bose-Einstein condensate (BEC).
  - Near critical temperature for condensation, many-body effects significantly influence the system.
  - Transition to classical Boltzmann gas behavior occurs only in a nondegenerate bath.
- **Experimental Setup**: Utilized a box-trapped Bose gas of 39K atoms, varying temperature and density, and employed rf injection spectroscopy to map impurity spectra.
- **Theoretical Framework**: Results aligned with the ideal Bose polaron theory, demonstrating strong qualitative agreement.
- **Significance**: Enhanced understanding of impurity dynamics in quantum many-body systems, contributing to the broader field of condensed matter physics.
