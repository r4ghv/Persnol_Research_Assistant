# Summary

- **Study Focus**: Investigated mobile impurities in a thermal Bose gas, analyzing the effects of temperature and impurity-bath interactions.
  
- **Key Findings**:
  - Spectra narrow with increasing temperature for strong impurity-bath interactions.
  - Impurity energy shift is suppressed compared to a quasipure Bose-Einstein condensate (BEC).
  - Near the critical temperature for condensation, many-body effects are significant, with the system approaching classical Boltzmann behavior only in a nondegenerate bath.

- **Theoretical Framework**: 
  - Key spectral features align with the ideal Bose polaron theory.
  - The study includes a comparison of experimental results with theoretical predictions, showing qualitative agreement.

- **Experimental Setup**:
  - Utilized a gas of 39K atoms in an optical box trap, with tunable temperature and density.
  - Employed rf injection spectroscopy to map impurity spectra across varying conditions.

- **Significance**: 
  - Provides insights into the complex behavior of impurities in quantum systems, contributing to the understanding of many-body physics and polaron dynamics in bosonic environments.
